(https://github.com/paper-design/shaders), Copyright Paper Design,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Ported shaders (Sources/Shaders/): Mesh Gradient, Neuro Noise (original
algorithm by @zozuar), Smoke Ring, Metaballs, Swirl, Spiral, Warp,
Grain Gradient, Dot Orbit, God Rays, Water, Voronoi, Color Panels,
Liquid Metal, Gem Smoke. Shared helpers in the shader prelude
(hashes, value noise, simplex noise, banding fix) are also derived from
Paper Shaders. The ports translate the original GLSL to Metal Shading
Language and replace texture-based randomizers with procedural hashes.
